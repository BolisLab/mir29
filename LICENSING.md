# Licensing

This is a private pre-release repository. An open-source licence for original
code and a reuse licence for original documentation will be selected by the
authors before public release. The absence of a licence is not a grant of reuse
permission. Third-party code, annotations and data remain subject to their
original licences and access conditions. In particular, controlled-access
GTEx/dbGaP phenotypes are not redistributed here.

Before release, inventory third-party components and retain their notices.
Do not apply a blanket licence to external datasets or software.
